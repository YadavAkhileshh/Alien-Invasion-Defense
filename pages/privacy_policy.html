Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Alien Invasion Defense is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information to provide you with the best gaming experience:

  • Game Statistics: High scores, level progress, and gameplay preferences stored locally on your device
  • Technical Data: Browser type and device information for compatibility purposes
  • Usage Analytics: Basic visitor count and page views to improve the game

How We Use Your Information

  • Save your game progress and high scores locally
  • Provide personalized gaming experience
  • Improve game performance and compatibility
  • Maintain visitor statistics

Data Storage

All game data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This includes:

  • High scores and achievements
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Theme selection (dark/light mode)

No personal data is transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: For typography (no personal data collected)
  • Font Awesome: For icons (no personal data collected)
  • Netlify: For hosting (standard web server logs)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your game data at any time through browser settings
  • Disable cookies and local storage
  • Contact us with privacy concerns
