Common Feedback Categories

Bug Reports

Found something not working correctly? Please include:

  • Browser and version
  • Device type (desktop/mobile)
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • Expected vs actual behavior

Feature Requests

Have ideas for new features? Consider:

  • How it would improve gameplay
  • Similar features in other games
  • Technical feasibility
  • Impact on existing players

General Feedback

Share your overall experience:

  • What you enjoyed most
  • Areas for improvement
  • Difficulty balance
  • User interface feedback

Development Roadmap

Based on community feedback, here are some areas we're considering for future updates:

  • Additional alien types and behaviors
  • New power-up varieties
  • Enhanced visual effects
  • Multiplayer capabilities
  • Achievement system
  • Custom control mapping

Response Time

We aim to respond to all feedback within:

  • Critical bugs: 24-48 hours
  • General issues: 3-5 business days
  • Feature requests: 1-2 weeks for initial review
  • General feedback: 1 week
