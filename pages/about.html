About Alien Invasion Defense

Mission Statement

Alien Invasion Defense is a thrilling space shooter game that puts you in command of Earth's last line of defense. Face waves of alien invaders with strategic gameplay, power-ups, and intense combat action.

Game Features

  • Dynamic Gameplay: Fast-paced action with increasing difficulty
  • Multiple Alien Types: Each with unique behaviors and point values
  • Power-Up System: Health boosts, shields, and speed enhancements
  • Progressive Levels: Challenging gameplay that scales with your skill
  • High Score System: Compete with yourself and others
  • Mobile Friendly: Play on desktop or mobile devices
