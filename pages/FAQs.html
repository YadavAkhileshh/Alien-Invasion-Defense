Frequently Asked Questions

Game Basics

How do I play the game?

Use arrow keys or A/D to move your spaceship left and right. Press spacebar or click to shoot at the alien invaders. Avoid letting aliens reach the bottom of the screen.

What are the different difficulty levels?

Easy mode has slower aliens and longer spawn intervals. Medium provides balanced gameplay. Hard mode features faster aliens with shorter spawn times for increased challenge.

How does scoring work?

Different alien types give different points: Default (10), Cute (15), Terrifying (20), Robotic (25), and Ghostly (30) aliens. Your score increases each level.

Power-ups and Features

What power-ups are available?

Health Boost restores one life, Speed Boost increases movement speed temporarily, and Shield provides temporary invincibility against alien attacks.

How do I save my progress?

Your high score and game settings are automatically saved in your browser's local storage. No account registration required.

Technical Support

The game won't load. What should I do?

Ensure you have a modern browser with JavaScript enabled. Clear your browser cache and refresh the page. Check your internet connection for audio files.

Can I play on mobile devices?

Yes, the game is fully responsive and includes touch controls for mobile devices. Touch the left/right sides to move and the center fire button to shoot.

Why is there no sound?

Check your browser's audio settings and the in-game volume controls. Some browsers require user interaction before playing audio.

Account and Data

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. All game data is stored locally on your device for privacy and convenience.

How do I reset my high score?

Clear your browser's local storage or use developer tools to remove the 'alienDefenseHighScore' key from localStorage.

Contact and Support

How can I report bugs or suggest features?

Visit our GitHub repository to report issues or suggest improvements. You can also contact the developer directly through the provided social links.

Is the game open source?

Yes, the complete source code is available on GitHub under the MIT license. Contributions are welcome from the community.
